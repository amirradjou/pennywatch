Every change of 0.5% or more between consecutive stable releases, with the runs of the two releases apart, and
every change in a gate count. Names are Catalyst versions, each with its matching PennyLane and Lightning.
Generated by:

```sh

```

*Confirmed* means a second, separate measurement showed the same change, the same way round and about the same size:
within a quarter of the step, or 0.25 points. The first measurement came from GitHub's runners and the second from a
laptop, and *Re-measured* is the change the laptop saw.

because the laptop doesn't agree with the runners as closely as runners agree with each other. On instructions the two
differ by 0.03% typically and up to 0.94%, on `qpe/lightning` at 0.10.0. On peak memory they differ by up to 2.8%,
while four runners measuring 0.15.0 agreed to 0.09%. So builds are only compared runner with runner.
